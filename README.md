# 📊 Retail Sales Analysis Dashboard (Excel)

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing **retail sales data** and building **interactive dashboards in Microsoft Excel** to transform raw transactional data into meaningful business insights.
The dashboards help stakeholders understand **sales performance across regions, categories, states, and salespersons**, enabling data-driven decision-making.

---

## 📊 Dashboards Created

### 1️⃣ Retail Sales Overview Dashboard
  Dashboard_1.png
**Key Metrics & Visuals:**

* Total Sales
* Total Orders
* Average Order Value (AOV)
* Total Items Sold
* Monthly Sales Trend
* Category-wise Sales Distribution

**Purpose:**
Provides a high-level view of overall business performance and sales trends.

---

### 2️⃣ Salesperson Performance Dashboard

**Key Metrics & Analysis:**

* Individual salesperson sales contribution
* KPI-based performance comparison
* Top-performing salesperson identification

**Interactivity:**

* Slicers for:

  * Region
  * Category
  * Salesperson

**Purpose:**
Helps evaluate and compare salesperson performance effectively.

---

### 3️⃣ Discount & State-wise Analysis Dashboard

**Key Insights & Visuals:**

* State-wise sales performance
* Discount percentage vs sales correlation (scatter chart)
* Identification of high-performing states and regions

**Purpose:**
Analyzes the impact of discounts on sales and highlights geographically strong markets.

---

## 🛠 Tools & Techniques Used

* Microsoft Excel
* Pivot Tables & Pivot Charts
* Slicers & Filters
* Data Cleaning & Formatting
* KPI Identification
* Dashboard Design & Data Storytelling

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

* Certain regions contribute a significantly higher share of total sales
* A small group of top-performing salespersons generates a major portion of revenue
* Higher discounts do **not always** lead to higher sales
* Category-level analysis reveals high-demand product segments
